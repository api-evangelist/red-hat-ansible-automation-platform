In the dynamic digital landscape of the Middle East, we are seeing a shift from AI experimentation to production-ready innovation. This transition is becoming a key factor in driving national competitiveness and economic resilience across the region.The opportunity ahead is significant. Projections suggest AI could contribute USD 320 billion to the Middle East’s economy by 2030, with Egypt expected to generate nearly 8% of its GDP from AI in the same timeframe.However, capturing this value requires navigating a marketplace defined by contrast.
